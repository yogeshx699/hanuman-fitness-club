About Us

At Hanuman Fitness Club, we believe that fitness is a journey of self‑love. Located in the heart of Uslapur, our studio offers a warm, non‑intimidating environment where women (and men!) can achieve their goals.

Our trainers are dedicated to helping you find strength, flexibility, and peace – all while having fun. We’re more than a gym; we’re a community.
